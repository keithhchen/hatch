# Maya Chen

Maya is a career educator and resume coach with an existing paid course and a
library of past reviews. Her customers value how she selects evidence, orders
arguments, removes unsupported claims, and distinguishes a stronger resume from
one that merely sounds more senior.

For this demonstration, Maya wants to publish **Signal Resume Review**: a
39-dollar, per-review Creator Agent that reads a buyer's resume, target role,
and supporting evidence, then produces an evidence-grounded revision plan and
usable rewrites. It must not invent metrics, imply unsupported ownership, or
promise an interview or job outcome.

Maya supplies her existing course, PDF workbook, lesson recording, transcript,
examples, and prior feedback as they already exist. She does not author a
prompt, schema, RAG manifest, claim map, or Eval suite. Before publishing, she
reviews the running product and representative behavior—not Factory internals.
