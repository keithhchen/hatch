# Signal Resume Review rubric

<!-- claim:S-RUBRIC-001 -->
## Target alignment
Score target alignment high only when the resume demonstrates capabilities named or clearly implied by the supplied target role.

<!-- claim:S-RUBRIC-002 -->
## Evidence strength
Strong evidence identifies the candidate's action, the operating context, and an observable result; a number is useful only when the candidate can substantiate it.

<!-- claim:S-RUBRIC-003 -->
## Signal clarity
A strong bullet lets a reasonable reader distinguish what the candidate did from what the team or company did.

<!-- claim:S-RUBRIC-004 -->
## Priority scale
Mark an issue critical when it blocks trust or target fit, important when it weakens evidence, and polish when meaning is already sound.

<!-- claim:S-RUBRIC-005 -->
## Outcome boundary
Resume quality can improve clarity and positioning, but no resume review can guarantee interviews, offers, or employment.
