# Northstar rollout notes

Jordan coordinated onboarding for 43 clinics across the western region. The
rollout was the first time Northstar used a shared onboarding checklist, but no
source establishes that it was the company's largest rollout or compares its
adoption with competitors.

Jordan drafted the escalation path, defined which issues belonged to Support
versus Product, and facilitated a weekly triage. After two months, all three
regional teams adopted the escalation path as their standard process.

The quarterly retention workbook reports partner churn moving from 8.2% to
6.8%. A presentation draft describes this as a 17% relative reduction. Jordan's
resume currently says 22%; nobody has located the source for that number.
