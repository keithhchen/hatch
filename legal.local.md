# Test Contract Review Playbook

This file is a local test fixture for the review-contract workflow. It is not
legal advice and must be reviewed by qualified counsel before use.

## Customer SaaS positions

- Customer Data: provider may process Customer Data only to provide and secure the service. No training, commercialization, sale, or unrelated product improvement without Customer's written consent.
- Data protection: require a DPA, appropriate security controls, prompt breach notice, subprocessors notice and objection rights, and lawful cross-border transfer safeguards.
- IP: each party keeps pre-existing IP. Customer owns Customer Data, customer materials, and customer-specific deliverables; provider receives only the license needed to provide the service.
- Indemnity: provider gives IP infringement and provider-caused data breach or law violation indemnity. Customer indemnity is limited to customer-provided materials and unlawful use.
- Liability: target a mutual cap of at least twelve months of fees, with separate higher caps or carve-outs for confidentiality, data protection, IP infringement, fraud, and willful misconduct.
- Renewal: annual renewal requires at least 90 days' notice and renewal price increases should be capped or subject to consent.
- Termination and deletion: customer needs termination rights for uncured material breach and convenience where commercially available, transition assistance, export in a usable format, and deletion certification after the retention period.
