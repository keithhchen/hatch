# Acme Analytics SaaS Subscription Agreement

This SaaS Subscription Agreement is entered into by and between Acme Analytics, Inc. ("Provider") and Northstar Retail Group, Inc. ("Customer").

## 1. Services

Provider will make its hosted analytics platform available to Customer for internal business analytics. Provider may modify, suspend, or discontinue any feature at any time without liability.

## 2. Term and Renewal

The initial term is three years. The agreement automatically renews for successive one-year terms unless Customer gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees

Annual subscription fees are payable in advance and are non-refundable. Provider may increase fees by up to 12% at each renewal.

## 4. Customer Data

Customer grants Provider a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable license to use Customer Data to operate, improve, train, benchmark, and commercialize Provider products and services. Provider may use aggregated or de-identified Customer Data for any business purpose.

## 5. Data Protection

Provider will use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect Customer Data. Provider may use subcontractors without prior notice. Provider will notify Customer of a confirmed security breach without undue delay and in any event within 15 business days after confirmation.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party's confidential information using reasonable care. Confidentiality obligations survive for two years after termination. Trade secrets are protected only during that two-year period.

## 7. Intellectual Property

Provider owns the platform and all improvements, feedback, configurations, reports, dashboards, templates, and derivative works created during the subscription, including those based on Customer requirements or Customer Data.

## 8. Warranties and Disclaimer

Provider warrants that the platform will materially conform to its documentation. Provider's sole obligation for any breach is to use commercially reasonable efforts to correct the nonconformity. Except as expressly stated, the services are provided "as is".

## 9. Indemnification

Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold Provider harmless from any third-party claim arising from Customer Data, Customer's use of the services, or Customer's breach of this agreement. Provider has no indemnification obligation for intellectual property infringement, data breach, or violation of law.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

Neither party will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages. Provider's aggregate liability under this agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the one month preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The liability cap applies to all claims, including confidentiality breaches, data security incidents, indemnification obligations, and gross negligence.

## 11. Termination

Provider may terminate immediately if Customer fails to pay any amount when due. Customer may terminate only after Provider fails to cure a material breach within 60 days after written notice. Upon termination, Provider may delete Customer Data after 10 days.

## 12. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York.
