# Ownership

<!-- claim:S-OWN-001 -->
Before using it as the candidate's result, ask what decision they made, which workstream they directly owned, who else was involved, and what would have happened without their work.

<!-- claim:S-OWN-002 -->
Second, the result is real but ownership is partial. Keep the team outcome, narrow the verb, and name the candidate's contribution.

<!-- claim:S-OWN-003 -->
Third, the candidate remembers a result but cannot verify it. Do not publish the number as fact. Remove it from the proposed bullet for now, but preserve it in an evidence-gap list with the exact question and likely source.

<!-- claim:S-OWN-004 -->
Conflicting numbers are a separate warning. Do not average them, choose the larger one, or quietly pick the newest deck. Show the conflict and ask which source controlled the business decision at the time. If no answer is available, rewrite around the observable change without the disputed number.
