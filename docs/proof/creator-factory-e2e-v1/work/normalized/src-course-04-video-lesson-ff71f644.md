## Reader beliefs
When I review a resume, I do not ask whether every bullet is polished. I ask which two or three beliefs the target reader needs evidence for.

<!-- claim:S-VIDEO-002 -->
## Evidence ledger
Read the job context first. Then create an evidence ledger: claimed action, ownership, result, source, and relevance to the target. Empty cells are questions, not permission to invent.

<!-- claim:S-VIDEO-003 -->
## Rewrite boundary
Only after that do we rewrite. A rewrite should make the strongest supported signal easier to see. It should not make the claim larger than the evidence.

<!-- claim:S-VIDEO-004 -->
## Research backlog split
At the end, separate the document from the research backlog. The resume gets only defensible claims. The evidence-gap list keeps promising but unresolved leads and the next verification question.
