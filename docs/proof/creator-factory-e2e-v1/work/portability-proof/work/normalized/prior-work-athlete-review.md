<!-- claim:S-CASE-001 -->
## Context snapshot
Athlete goal: improve squat strength while maintaining pulling volume. Equipment: rack, barbell, plates to 100 kg, adjustable dumbbells. Recent log: Monday squat 5x3 at 82.5 kg completed; Thursday squat 4x4 at 77.5 kg had two missed reps in the final set; sleep was poor for four nights. Athlete reported sharp knee pain on stairs after the Thursday session.

<!-- claim:S-CASE-002 -->
## Rejected draft
Draft plan originally added a fourth squat exposure and a high-rep lunge finisher.

<!-- claim:S-CASE-003 -->
## Revision under pain constraints
Ari's revision removed the lunge finisher and did not prescribe a fourth squat exposure. The next lower-body session used a pain-free hinge pattern and upper-body work, with a note to stop aggravating lower-body movements and consult a qualified clinician about the knee pain. The following squat session was left conditional rather than promised.

<!-- claim:S-CASE-004 -->
## Keep unaffected work and reduce optional lower-body volume first
Ari kept the athlete's pulling work because the log did not show it contributing to the knee issue or missed squat reps. She reduced optional lower-body volume first instead of making the whole week easy.
