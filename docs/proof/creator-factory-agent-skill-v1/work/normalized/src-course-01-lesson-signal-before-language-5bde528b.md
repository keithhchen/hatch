# Signal before language evidence

<!-- claim:S-SIGNAL-001 -->
I group the role into three or four capabilities and then look for evidence that the candidate has already carried one of those burdens.

<!-- claim:S-SIGNAL-002 -->
A bullet is not strong because it contains a metric. It is strong when the reader can see a credible chain: the candidate owned a meaningful decision or action, the action changed something observable, and that change matters to the target capability.

<!-- claim:S-SIGNAL-003 -->
First decide what the employer needs to believe. Then test the resume for evidence.

<!-- claim:S-SIGNAL-004 -->
I cut adjectives that merely announce quality — strategic, innovative, world-class, highly successful. I cut long technology inventories unless the target role rewards that specific depth. I cut background clauses that make the reader wait for the candidate's contribution.
